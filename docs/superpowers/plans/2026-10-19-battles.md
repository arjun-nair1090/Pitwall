> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A new "Overtakes and battles" page for any race or sprint. It shows a lap chart of every driver's position, every on-track pass, each driver's places gained at the start and passes made and lost, and every sustained fight within a second.

**Architecture:** A pure-pandas service (`race_battles.py`) reads the session's laps (position and lap-end time per lap) into one payload: drivers, overtakes and battles. One POST endpoint serves it with a 600 s memo. The frontend is a new `/battles` module with four panels: lap chart, drivers table, passes feed and battles table.

**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, FastF1 3.8.3, pandas, pytest; Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind, Recharts, Vitest, Testing Library.

**Spec:** None. The user asked for an "overtake & battle detector" (feature 7 of 8) and skipped the design conversation. The Definitions below are the authority.

## Definitions

FastF1 data: `laps.Position` is the race position at the end of each lap. `laps.Time` is the session time at the end of the lap. A pass and re-pass within one lap is invisible, and the page says so.

- **Session:** races (`R`) and sprints (`S`) only. Anything else is a 422 with `Overtakes need a race or a sprint.`
- **Total laps:** `int(laps["LapNumber"].max())`.
- **Position of a driver on lap n:** their `Position` at `LapNumber == n`, or `None` when missing. Positions are 1-based integers.
- **Pit lap:** the lap has a non-null `PitInTime` (in-lap) or `PitOutTime` (out-lap).
- **Neutralised lap:** that driver's `TrackStatus` on that lap contains any of `4`, `5`, `6`, `7` (SC, red, VSC, VSC ending). This is the same rule as `corner_analysis.clean_laps`.
- **Overtake on lap n (n ≥ 2):** A is behind B at the end of lap n−1 (A's position number is larger) and ahead at the end of lap n. Five conditions:
  - all four positions exist;
  - lap n is not a pit lap for A;
  - lap n is not a pit lap for B;
  - lap n is not neutralised for A;
  - lap n is not neutralised for B.

  Every such pair is one overtake, so one car passing two others on a lap counts twice. `position` is A's position at the end of lap n.

  Lap 1 is the start and is counted separately.
- **Start gain:** `grid − position on lap 1`, from `session.results.GridPosition`.
  - `grid` is `None` when GridPosition is missing or 0 (a pit-lane start), and then `start_gain` is `None`.
  - Positive means places gained.
- **Made / lost:** counts of overtakes where the driver is A / B.
- **Gap to the car ahead on lap n:** `Time(driver, n) − Time(car whose position is driver's − 1 on lap n, n)` in seconds. It is undefined for P1, or when either time is missing.
- **Battle lap:** lap n where all of these hold:
  - the driver's gap to the car directly ahead is under `BATTLE_GAP_S = 1.0` (the DRS window);
  - lap n is not a pit lap for either car;
  - lap n is not neutralised for either car.
- **Battle:** a run of consecutive battle laps with the same chaser and the same car ahead, of at least `MIN_BATTLE_LAPS = 3` laps.
  - `start_lap`, `end_lap`, `laps` (count), `min_gap` (smallest gap in the run).
  - `outcome`, judged on lap `end_lap + 1`, first match wins:
    1. `"flag"`: `end_lap` is the last lap either car completed.
    2. `"pits"`: lap `end_lap + 1` is a pit lap for either car.
    3. `"passed"`: on lap `end_lap + 1` the chaser is ahead of the leader (an overtake by the definition above). `outcome_lap = end_lap + 1`.
    4. `"neutralised"`: lap `end_lap + 1` is neutralised for either car.
    5. `"dropped"`: anything else (the gap opened, or another car came between).

    `outcome_lap` is `end_lap + 1` for `passed`, `pits` and `neutralised`. It is `end_lap` for `flag`. It is `None` for `dropped`.
- **Ordering:**
  - drivers by finishing position from `session.results.Position` (None last, then code);
  - overtakes by lap, then `position`;
  - battles by `laps` descending, then `start_lap`, then chaser code.
- **Memo:** 600 s, keyed `(year, round or gp, session)`, with `clear_cache()`, the same shape as `race_pace`.

## Payload (POST /api/v1/telemetry/race-battles, body `{year, gp="", round, session="R"}`)

The endpoint returns this object bare, with no `{data: …}` envelope:

```json
{
  "total_laps": 44,
  "drivers": [{
    "code": "VER", "name": "Max Verstappen", "team": "Red Bull Racing", "color": "#3671C6",
    "grid": 11, "finish": 4, "start_gain": 3, "made": 3, "lost": 0,
    "positions": [8, 7, 7, 6]
  }],
  "overtakes": [{"lap": 12, "driver": "VER", "passed": "MAG", "position": 10}],
  "battles": [{"chaser": "NOR", "leader": "VER", "start_lap": 35, "end_lap": 44, "laps": 10,
               "min_gap": 0.426, "outcome": "flag", "outcome_lap": 44}]
}
```

`positions` has exactly `total_laps` entries, for laps 1…total, with `null` where the driver has no lap (retired). Every number is a built-in int or float.

## Review Focus

1. **A driver who pits** must never be credited with a pass by the cars that move up while they're in the lane. The pit-lap exclusion applies to both cars, on the in-lap and the out-lap. Pinned in Task 1.
2. **Safety-car laps:** passes and battles stop under a safety car or VSC. When the field bunches up behind the safety car, that must not become a "battle". Pinned in Task 1.
3. **A retirement:** a driver whose positions end early has `null` positions afterwards, can't be "passed" by everyone behind once they stop, and their battle ends `"flag"`. Pinned in Task 1.
4. **A pit-lane start** (grid 0) has `grid: null` and `start_gain: null`. The page shows "Pit lane", not "+19". Pinned in Tasks 1 and 3.
5. **Teammates on the lap chart** share a colour, so the second is dashed (`seriesDash`), and the highlight makes one driver readable among 20 lines. Pinned in Task 4.
