# Race Pace and Degradation Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A new "Race pace" page that ranks every driver by fuel-corrected race pace, shows each driver's stints lap by lap with a degradation slope per stint, and gives a field-wide degradation curve per tyre compound.

**Architecture:** A pure-pandas service (`race_pace.py`) turns a race or sprint's laps into one payload: fuel model, drivers (ranked, with stints and clean laps), compounds (pooled slope and a measured curve). One POST endpoint serves it with a 600 s memo. The frontend is a new `/pace` module with three panels: ranking table, stint chart for one driver, compound curves.

**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, FastF1 3.8.3, pandas, numpy, pytest; Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind, Recharts, Vitest, Testing Library.

**Spec:** None. The user asked for "fuel-corrected race pace & degradation curves" (feature 6 of 8) and skipped the design conversation. The Definitions below are the authority.

## Definitions

- **Session:** races (`R`) and sprints (`S`) only. Any other session code is a 422 with the message `Race pace needs a race or a sprint.`
- **Total laps:** `int(laps["LapNumber"].max())` for the session.
- **Fuel model** (stated, not fitted):
  - `SECONDS_PER_KG = 0.03`.
  - Start fuel `FUEL_START_KG = 110.0` from 2019, `105.0` before (the FIA limit).
  - A sprint runs about a third of a race distance on the same lap, so it burns the same per lap: `DISTANCE_FACTOR = {"R": 1.0, "S": 3.05}` (305 km race / 100 km sprint).
  - `kg_per_lap = start_kg / (total_laps * DISTANCE_FACTOR[session])`.
  - `seconds_per_lap = SECONDS_PER_KG * kg_per_lap`.
  - Corrected time of lap `n`: `lap_time - seconds_per_lap * (total_laps - n)`. In words: every lap as if run on the last lap's fuel load. The final lap is unchanged and earlier laps get quicker.
- **Clean lap:** `corner_analysis.clean_laps` exactly, applied per driver:
  - timed; not an in or out lap; not lap 1; not deleted;
  - no TrackStatus containing 4, 5, 6 or 7;
  - within 107% of that driver's best.

  Reuse it; don't copy it.
- **Stint:** FastF1's `Stint` column, per driver.
  - Rows with a NaN `Stint` are ignored.
  - `start_lap` and `end_lap` are the min and max `LapNumber` over all of the driver's laps in that stint, not only the clean ones.
  - `compound` is the first non-null `Compound` in the stint, or `"UNKNOWN"`.
  - A stint with no clean laps is still listed, with `laps: []`.
- **Stint degradation** (`deg_per_lap`): the least-squares slope of corrected time against `TyreLife` over the stint's clean laps.
  - Needs at least `MIN_STINT_LAPS = 5` clean laps with a non-null `TyreLife` and at least 2 distinct `TyreLife` values; otherwise `None`.
  - Not clamped: a negative slope is reported as measured.
  - `fit` is the fitted line evaluated at the first and last clean lap (`{"from": {"lap", "time"}, "to": {"lap", "time"}}`), or `None` when `deg_per_lap` is `None`. Within a stint, lap number and tyre life move together, so the line drawn against lap number has the same slope.
- **Race pace:** the median corrected time over a driver's clean laps.
  - Needs at least `MIN_PACE_LAPS = 5` clean laps, otherwise `pace: None`.
  - `gap` is pace minus the best pace in the session (`None` when pace is `None`).
  - Drivers are sorted by pace ascending; `None` comes last, ordered by code.
  - If no driver has a pace, raise `SessionDataError("No driver has enough clean laps in this session to measure race pace.")`.
- **Compound degradation**, pooled across the field, per compound:
  - Use only stints whose `deg_per_lap` is not `None`.
  - `deg_per_lap` is the within-stint (fixed-effects) slope, `Σ_s Σ (x − x̄_s)(y − ȳ_s) / Σ_s Σ (x − x̄_s)²`, where x is `TyreLife` and y is corrected time.
  - Each stint's own intercept is `ȳ_s − slope · x̄_s`. Each lap's loss is `y − intercept_s`, the time lost against fresh tyres.
  - `curve` is the median loss per integer tyre life, keeping only tyre lives with at least `MIN_CURVE_LAPS = 3` laps: `[{"tyre_life", "loss", "laps"}]`, ascending.
  - A compound with no qualifying stints is left out.
  - Compounds are ordered SOFT, MEDIUM, HARD, INTERMEDIATE, WET, then the rest alphabetically.
- **Memo:** 600 s, keyed on `(year, round or gp, session)`, with `clear_cache()`. Same shape as `corner_analysis`.

## Payload (POST /api/v1/telemetry/race-pace, body `{year, gp="", round, session="R"}`)

```json
{
  "fuel": {"start_kg": 110.0, "kg_per_lap": 2.5, "seconds_per_kg": 0.03, "seconds_per_lap": 0.075, "total_laps": 44},
  "drivers": [{
    "code": "HAM", "name": "Lewis Hamilton", "team": "Mercedes", "color": "#27F4D2",
    "pace": 106.3, "gap": 0.0, "clean_laps": 39,
    "stints": [{
      "stint": 1, "compound": "MEDIUM", "start_lap": 1, "end_lap": 11,
      "deg_per_lap": 0.106, "fit": {"from": {"lap": 2, "time": 106.2}, "to": {"lap": 10, "time": 107.0}},
      "laps": [{"lap": 2, "time": 108.9, "corrected": 106.3, "tyre_life": 2}]
    }]
  }],
  "compounds": [{"compound": "MEDIUM", "deg_per_lap": 0.11, "stints": 30, "laps": 260,
                 "curve": [{"tyre_life": 2, "loss": 0.21, "laps": 18}]}]
}
```

Field types:
- `tyre_life`: int or null.
- `stint`, `start_lap`, `end_lap` and `lap`: int.
- Every time is in seconds as a float.

## Review Focus

1. **A driver who retires early** (fewer than 5 clean laps): listed with `pace: None` after the ranked drivers, never ranked first, and never crashing the slope maths. Pinned in Task 1.
2. **A stint of only 1 to 4 clean laps, or with a constant TyreLife** (FastF1 sometimes repeats tyre life after a red flag): `deg_per_lap` is `None` and there is no `polyfit` RankWarning or crash. Pinned in Task 1.
3. **A wet or mixed race**: INTERMEDIATE and WET stints flow through. Compound order is SOFT, MEDIUM, HARD, INTERMEDIATE, WET. An unknown compound shows as "Unknown" and doesn't crash `getCompoundStyle`. Pinned in Tasks 1 and 3.
4. **A sprint**: the fuel correction per lap is the race's per-lap rate (factor 3.05), not three times bigger. Pinned in Task 1.
5. **Changing session quickly**: the page never shows the previous race's numbers under the new race's heading. `useAsync`'s key covers this; the meta line names the analysed session. Pinned in Task 3.
